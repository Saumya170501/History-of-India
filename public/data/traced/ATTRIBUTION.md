# Map credits

Traced shapes are approximate. They are a reading of one Commons file, not a surveyed frontier. Low confidence means the placement or the source is loose, and the edge is drawn feathered.

## Mauryan core

- Commons file: [File:Mauryan Empire map.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mauryan_Empire_map.svg)
- Author: MaxxL (Maxxl2)
- Licence of the file: public domain
- The file’s own source: the page says “Atlas” and does not name it
- Depicted date: the file prints no year. Used for the cited Maurya window, 321–185 BCE
- Confidence: low
- What was traced: the gold fill only. The cyan shapes on the same file were not included. City labels were fitted to modern coordinates and miss by about one to two degrees. The ring is cut to a Natural Earth land mask.
