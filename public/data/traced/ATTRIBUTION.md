# Map credits

Traced shapes are approximate. They are a reading of one Commons file, not a surveyed frontier. The fit error is the confidence: higher means the coastline fit was within about half a degree.

## Mauryan core

- Commons file: [File:Mauryan Empire map.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mauryan_Empire_map.svg)
- Author: MaxxL (Maxxl2)
- Licence of the file: Public domain
- The file’s own source: The file page’s own source line says “Atlas” and does not name the atlas or a plate.
- Depicted date: The file does not print a year. It is a general political map of the Mauryan Empire, shown here across the cited Maurya window 321–185 BCE.
- Confidence: low
- What was traced: Only the gold fill is traced. A separate cyan fill on the same file, read as tributary or other territory, is left out. City-label positions were fitted to the modern coordinates of Pataliputra, Taxila, Ujjain, Mathura, Sanchi and Bodh Gaya. The fit misses those cities by about one to two degrees. The ring is cut to a Natural Earth land mask.
