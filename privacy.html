Privacy

No account, no tracking, no cookies.

The atlas does not ask you to sign in. It does not set cookies, and it does not run analytics or advertising.

Feedback

The feedback form does not send anything to a server. Choosing Send opens your own email app with a message addressed to the atlas author. The message includes what you typed, the page you were on, and the year if one was open.

Name and email are optional. They are included in that email only if you fill them in. If you do not send the email, nothing leaves your device. This site does not store the form.

The map may request tiles from OpenHistoricalMap while you use the atlas. That request is made by your browser to OpenHistoricalMap, under their own terms. This site does not add a tracker to it.
