# Historical atlas research: 1 CE — evidence baseline

Status: **research draft** (2026-11-08). This is not a complete political map.

## Rules for rendering 1 CE

- Display **1 CE**, not year zero; avoid extrapolating modern state borders into antiquity.
- Distinguish `attested_site`, `regional_polity`, and `hypothesized_extent`.
- Do **not** draw precise polygon boundaries from broad dynasty-period summaries.
- Do not assign a named ruler to 1 CE merely because their dynasty existed in the first century.
- For disputed chronology, show a range and uncertainty label; absence of evidence is not evidence of uninhabited land.
