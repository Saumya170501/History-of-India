## Coverage queue

1. 1–300 CE: regional Satavahana, Kushan, Shaka/Kshatrapa and southern polities; resolve the precise 1 CE pilot.
2. 300–750: Gupta and regional successors, Vakataka, Pallava, early Chalukya and other contemporary powers.
3. 750–1200: Pala, Gurjara-Pratihara, Rashtrakuta, Chola, Chalukya, Paramara, Chandella, Kashmir and eastern/northeastern dynasties.
4. 1200–1526: Delhi Sultanate plus contemporary regional and northeastern kingdoms, Vijayanagara and Deccan sultanates.
5. 1526–1750: Mughal and Sur reigns alongside Rajput, Deccan, Ahom, Maratha, Sikh and other regional histories.
6. 1750–1947: successor states, princely states, colonial administrations, resistance and political change.
7. 1947–2026: constitutional and administrative history under a separate modern-government model; do not label elected leaders as kings.

These are research headings, not verified dates or an exhaustive list of states. Research overlapping regions in parallel chronologically rather than treating one dynasty as ruler of all India. Track missing, disputed and corroborated facts explicitly. No completed coverage percentage is claimed without a defensible inventory.
